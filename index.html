Gate 2Teaching Professional

Requirements: a professional golf coaching license, in date, and eligibility, as set out on SGF licenses →. No course-by-course equivalency; the SGF interview is the assessment

+ SGF interview tests how you coach
Any professional golf coaching license applies. Examples:
  • Membership of a national PGA, e.g., GB&I, America, Canada, Australia, South Africa, Japan, India, Pakistan, Philippines
  • LPGA Class A
  • State or federation professional titles, e.g., Spain Técnico Deportivo Superior, China CGA Senior or National
Why an interview: teaching skill varies even among well-qualified coaches, so the interview tests how you coach, whatever your syllabus covered

Which door should a coach pick?

  • PGA for a facility career or moves abroad; men and women
  • LPGA for a faster teaching credential, mostly online, with no facility post required; women
  • Both later: PGA Class A members can add LPGA Class A for ~SAR 5.8k
Bottom line: both lead to the same SGF license through the same interview. PGA is the default for a facility career or moves abroad, LPGA the faster teaching-only door.

CPD renews both SGF licenses: 15 hours a year, mostly online and free

15 hours a year is the right bar: it matches The PGA, and the hours are free and online, so it is achievable from anywhere in the Kingdom.

What it does

Renews both two-year SGF licenses: 15+ hours in each CPD year (12 months from the license start date), with proof: a certificate, attendance record or provider confirmation, uploaded on Riyadi.

What it does not do

Hours never turn a Community Instructor into a Teaching Professional. Gate 2 needs a professional golf coaching license and the SGF interview.

If it is missed

The license lapses until the shortfall is closed, and the coach cannot coach at SGF-affiliated facilities.
